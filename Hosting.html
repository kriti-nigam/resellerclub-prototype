FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is the hosting fully white-label?
Yes. Your brand is on the storefront, the control panel your customers see, and the invoices you send. You set the retail price, the customer relationship stays with you, and your customers never see ResellerClub.
Who supports my customers?
Your customers call you; our team backs you. You stay the face of your business, with 24/7 reseller-grade technical support behind you for the questions you want help with.
Can I move my existing customers' sites over?
Yes. Hands-on migration and onboarding support helps you move existing customer sites onto the platform, so you can bring a book of business with you.
Where are the servers located?
Every hosting type is available from two server locations, India and the US. Pick the one closest to your customer's audience.
How does wholesale pricing work?
Every plan has a wholesale price, listed on the Pricing page, and you set your own retail price on top. The margin between the two is yours, on every renewal.
Do I get an account manager?
Yes. A dedicated account manager comes with the platform, and they do more than manage the account. On volume, they can work out discounts for you, and they act as a business consultant as you grow your hosting business.

# ResellerClub Positioning Brief

One page. For executive review. Evidence in [[Competitive Positioning Teardown]].

## The Position

> ResellerClub is the wholesale domain registrar built for the businesses that put others online.

We serve web hosts, web developers and agencies, and the vibe-coding and website-builder platforms shaping how sites get made. The job we own: provision and manage domains, plus the email, SSL, and hosting their customers need, without becoming a registrar.

ResellerClub is built for resellers. Your customers stay yours. We give you the wholesale economics and tooling to sell domains and adjacent services under your own brand.

### Positioning statement (internal)

For web hosts, developers, and AI website-builder platforms who need to provision and manage domains and related services for their own customers, ResellerClub is the wholesale domain registrar that combines reseller economics with a modern, agent-ready API, so they can sell and embed these services without becoming a registrar. Unlike the wholesale incumbents, ResellerClub is built for the people who build the web. Unlike the developer-first newcomers, ResellerClub is a true wholesale channel with the track record to back it.

## Why Now

Sachin Puri's growth bet is the wholesale channel, with ResellerClub as the engine on OrderBox. The current site leads with retail hosting and a buy-now flow. That undercuts the wholesale, developer-first identity we need. This is a return to roots: ResellerClub started as a domain registrar, and that is the ground we reclaim.

## The Opening

The market splits into two camps, and no one owns the middle.

| Camp | Owns | Misses |
|---|---|---|
| Wholesale incumbents (OpenSRS, Openprovider, Realtime Register) | Wholesale economics, reseller channel, scale | Silent on developers and AI agents |
| Builder and agent entrants (name.com, Cloudflare) | Developer experience, agent-ready, "where you build" | Not built as a true wholesale channel |

ResellerClub's space is the intersection: a wholesale registrar that builders and platforms can build on.

## Message Pillars

1. Wholesale, by heritage. Over 20 years as a domain reseller platform, on OrderBox, which powers 100+ ICANN-accredited registrars. Reseller-first pricing and control, white-label, and the registrar backbone run for you, so you scale without becoming a registrar.
2. Built for builders. Provision domain registrations and services directly on the front of your own site or app. Modern API, clean docs, fast time to market, and agent and MCP ready, so domains can be provisioned wherever your software and agents run.
3. Everything your customers need. Domains first, then email (Titan and Google Workspace), SSL, and hosting, under one account.
4. Support built for partners. 24/7 help, dedicated account management, hands-on migration and onboarding, and technical and API support. Reseller-grade people who understand the way you build and the customers depending on you, not a generic service desk.

## Product Hierarchy

Domains lead. Email, SSL, hosting, and ancillary services attach around them. The direct buy-now path stays but is de-emphasized. The primary call to action is to start reselling or to build on the API.

## Homepage Message (outline, not copy)

- Hero: ResellerClub as the wholesale domain registrar built for the people who build the web, naming the three audiences.
- Proof strip: 20+ years as a domain reseller platform and 100+ ICANN-accredited registrars on OrderBox, API and agent readiness, product breadth.
- Primary CTA: become a reseller. Secondary CTA: explore the API.

Dependency: the site launches in lockstep with the OrderBox API Refresh. The agent and MCP readiness claims go live only when those API items ship, so the brand never gets ahead of the product.

Ways to sell

Three ways to sell. One account.

Sell domains to your customers your way. Start with a ready storefront, manage through the control panel, or integrate the API into your own product. Pick one or mix all three.

No code

Storefront (Supersite)

Best for: Start selling fast with no engineering.

A hosted, branded storefront for your customers, built on the control panel. You set pricing and branding. We run the registrar backbone behind it.

You control: Branding and pricing.

Low code

Control panel

Best for: Day-to-day management without building software.

A web panel to manage domains, customers, orders, and DNS by hand. Good for support teams and smaller volumes.

You control: Operations and customer management.

Full integration

API

Best for: Building domains directly into your own product or platform.

A modern REST API to register, manage, and renew domains inside your own site, app, or workflow. Your interface, our backbone.

You control: The entire customer experience.

Storefront (Supersite)

A branded store, ready to sell.

A hosted storefront your customers buy from, in your branding and your pricing. We run the registrar backbone behind it, you keep the customer and the margin.

  • Your branding, your domain, your retail pricing
  • Customers buy and self-manage their domains
  • No engineering needed to launch
Control panel

Manage everything by hand.

A web panel to manage domains, customers, orders, and DNS. Built for support teams and day-to-day operations, with no software to build or maintain.

  • Domains, customers, orders, and DNS in one place
  • Good for support teams and smaller volumes
  • Powers the Storefront (Supersite) behind the scenes
API

Build it into your own product.

A modern REST API to register, manage, and renew domains inside your own site, app, or workflow. Your interface, our backbone. The full reference, sandbox, and SDKs live in the API and Developer Portal.

The ResellerClub logo lockup — mark + wordmark + optional mono product tag.

```jsx
<Logo tag="Developers" />
<Logo dark tag="API" />     {/* on ink surfaces */}
<Mark size={40} />          {/* mark alone, e.g. favicons / avatars */}
```

The mark is the official ResellerClub crown (`assets/resellerclub-mark.png`). Pass `src` if the asset sits elsewhere relative to your page. Keep the mono tag lowercase-ish and short ("Developers", "API", "Docs").

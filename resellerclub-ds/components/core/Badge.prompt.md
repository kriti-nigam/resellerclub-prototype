Small pill label for status, category, or a feature flag.

```jsx
<Badge tone="accent"><Icon name="sparkles" size={12} /> AI-ready</Badge>
<Badge tone="success">Live</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral` (default), `accent`, `success`, `teal`, `warning`, `danger`, `violet`, `dark`. Always pill-shaped; pair with an `Icon` for emphasis.
